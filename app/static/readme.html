End-to-end pipeline

From form submission to sales action

One structured company-research call and one compliance-reasoning call for each newly researched domain.

01
IN

Lead intake & validation

What happens

  • Accepts contact and company details
  • Validates required fields and email syntax
  • Compares work-email and website domains
  • Classifies company, free, or mismatched email
Business impact

Stops low-quality submissions early and gives sales cleaner contact data.

02
RS

Company research

What happens

  • Normalizes the company domain
  • Reuses cached research when available
  • Crawls up to 10 useful same-domain pages
  • Cleans navigation, scripts, and page noise
  • Builds one source-labelled evidence set
Business impact

Produces consistent lead context while reducing repeat work, cost, and response time.

03
FS

Fit scoring

100-point model

  • Company size: up to 35
  • Cloud intensity: up to 40
  • Business model: up to 15
  • Lead quality: up to 10
  • Every component includes a reason
Business impact

Creates transparent prioritization so sales can focus on likely cloud-cost opportunities.

04
CS

Compliance screening

What happens

  • Normalizes competitor names and suffixes
  • Calculates fuzzy name similarity
  • Reviews headquarters against policy rules
  • Returns pass, review, or block
  • Includes match, confidence, and reasoning
Business impact

Escalates ambiguous cases and reduces legal or commercial risk before outreach.

05
DR

Decision routing

Routing rules

  • Compliance block → Blocked
  • Compliance review → Manual review
  • Fit below 50 → Manual review
  • Free/mismatched email → Manual review
  • Otherwise → Sales ready
Business impact

Automates routine decisions while preserving human review where judgment matters.

06
OP

Persistence & action

What happens

  • Saves lead, profile, score, and decision
  • Sends the report through configured SMTP
  • Shows results in the expandable tracker
  • Supports email retries and CSV export
  • Records structured operational logs
Business impact

Creates an audit trail and gives sales immediate, reusable context for follow-up.

Cross-cutting safeguards

Reliability built into every stage

01

Website safety

Blocks localhost, private IP ranges, unsafe protocols, and redirects to internal addresses.

02

Structured output

Rejects malformed AI responses and validates every research and compliance result with Pydantic.

03

Model resilience

Retries once, then moves through the environment-configured OpenRouter fallback list.

04

Usage control

Caches research by normalized domain and targets only two reasoning operations for a new company.

05

Auditability

Logs crawl stages, model selection, token use, scoring, compliance, persistence, and notification status.

06

Secret handling

Keeps OpenRouter and SMTP credentials server-side and excludes them from browser responses and logs.

Business logic

Scoring informs priority.
Compliance controls access.

A company can be an excellent commercial fit and still be blocked. The system keeps opportunity scoring separate from risk screening, then combines both into a clear disposition.

Implementation choices

Small, explainable, and demo-ready

FastAPI + plain web UI

No frontend build pipeline is needed for a focused form and tracker.

SQLite instead of pgvector

The bounded evidence set fits in one prompt; semantic retrieval would add unnecessary infrastructure.

OpenRouter only

One centralized service handles model choice, fallback, retries, validation, and usage logging.

Deterministic scoring

The model extracts facts. Python applies visible rules so scores stay consistent and explainable.
