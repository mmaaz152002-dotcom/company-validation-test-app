How it works

Lead qualification workflow

How a form submission becomes a researched, scored, screened, and routed sales lead.

1

Lead intake and validation

  • Accepts contact and company information.
  • Validates required fields, email syntax, and website format.
  • Checks whether the work-email domain matches the company website.

Business value: removes invalid or low-quality submissions before sales spends time on them.

2

Company research

  • Normalizes the domain and checks cached research.
  • If needed, crawls up to 10 useful pages such as About, Products, Careers, Contact, and Legal.
  • Cleans page noise and creates one source-labelled evidence set.
  • Returns company size, headquarters, business model, cloud signals, confidence, and sources.

Business value: gives sales consistent company context while caching reduces repeat work and LLM usage.

3

Fit scoring

  • Company size contributes up to 35 points.
  • Cloud intensity contributes up to 40.
  • Business-model relevance contributes up to 15.
  • Lead quality contributes up to 10.
  • The result is low, borderline, promising, or strong.

Business value: creates a transparent priority order so sales can focus on likely cloud-cost opportunities.

4

Compliance screening

  • Normalizes company and competitor names and calculates fuzzy similarity.
  • Checks plausible competitor matches and headquarters-related policy risk.
  • Returns pass, review, or block with confidence and reasoning.

Business value: flags commercial or legal risk before the lead enters the active sales pipeline.

5

Decision routing

Blocked Compliance returns blockManual review Possible match, fit below 50, or non-company emailSales ready Compliance passes and all qualification gates pass

Business value: automates routine decisions while keeping a person involved for ambiguous cases.

6

Save, notify, and review

  • Saves the lead, company profile, score, compliance result, and disposition.
  • Sends the report to the selected notification email.
  • Shows the result in the expandable tracker and supports CSV export.
  • Records crawl, model, scoring, compliance, and email events in backend logs.

Business value: gives sales an immediate, auditable result they can review and act on.
